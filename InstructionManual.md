🧩 # Pattern Rush

## Research & Analysis
Pattern Rush is heavily inspired by classic attribute-matching games (most notably the tabletop game SET). It targets the brain's visual cortex and cognitive flexibility. Players must bypass their instinct to look for simple pairs and instead train their minds to process multiple variables simultaneously. The challenge scales not by changing the rules, but by overwhelming the player's working memory with visual data under time pressure.

## Instruction Manual

### Objective
Clear the board by identifying and selecting valid **Pattern Sets** before the timer expires.

### The Grid
You are presented with a dynamic grid of tiles. Each tile features four distinct attributes:

- **Shape** (e.g., Circle, Square, Triangle)
- **Color** (e.g., Red, Blue, Green)
- **Number** (1, 2, or 3 shapes per tile)
- **Shading** (Solid, Striped, Empty)

### How to Play

1. **Scan the Grid.** Look for a valid combination of three tiles.
2. **Verify the Rule.** Each attribute must be either all the same across the three tiles or completely different.
3. **Select.** Tap or click the three tiles to lock in your answer.
4. **Clear & Replace.** If correct, the tiles disappear, your score increases, and new tiles drop in to fill the void.

### Penalties
Guessing incorrectly or selecting an invalid set results in a **5-second time penalty**.

### Pro Tip
Focus on a single unique tile and scan for its exact opposite. Spotting three totally different tiles is often faster than verifying partial similarities.

⏱️ # Sequence Sprinter

## Research & Analysis
Sequence Sprinter shifts the focus from the visual cortex to the prefrontal cortex, testing algorithmic logic, arithmetic deduction, and working memory. The game draws from classic number theory—utilizing arithmetic progressions, geometric sequences, and Fibonacci-style scaling. The “sprint” element forces players to rely on rapid mental math and heuristic shortcuts rather than slow, methodical calculation.

## Instruction Manual

### Objective
Correctly deduce and complete as many logical sequences as possible to move your runner down the track before time runs out.

### The Track
You will see a sequence of numbers (or occasionally letters) with one missing element at the end or in the middle (e.g., `2, 6, 12, 20, _`).

### How to Play

1. **Analyze the Gap.** Look at the difference between the first few elements to deduce the governing rule. Possible rule families include:
   - **Simple Arithmetic:** Adding or subtracting a constant number.
   - **Geometric:** Multiplying or dividing by a constant.
   - **Progressive:** The gap itself increases (e.g., +2, +4, +6).
   - **Alternating:** Two different rules alternating step-by-step.
2. **Calculate.** Apply the rule to find the missing value.
3. **Submit.** Type your answer using the provided keypad and hit enter.
4. **Sprint.** A correct answer propels your sprinter forward, adds bonus seconds, and increases your streak multiplier.
5. **Stumble.** An incorrect answer breaks your streak, resets your multiplier to 1x, and deducts points.

### Pro Tip
Always check the difference between the numbers first. If the gaps still don’t add up, examine the difference between the gaps (the second derivative). When the numbers escalate quickly, switch your brain to multiplication or exponents.
